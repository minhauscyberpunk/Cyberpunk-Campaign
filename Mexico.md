# Description
Mexico is a country in [[North America]]. Mexico is a country of many biomes, from arid deserts in the north to rolling hills and mountains in the centre and dense tropical rainforest in the south.

The language spoken in Mexico is primarily [[Spanish]]. Mexico is the largest Spanish-speaking country in the world.

Mexico borders [[Central American Federation|the Central American Federation]] to the south, and both [[Texas|the Free State of Texas]] and [[NUSA|the NUSA]] to the north.
