The Time of the Red was the period of time following the Fourth Corporate War beginning in 2023 and lasting until the 2050s. During this time, dust, fill, soil, and debris kicked up from the [Night City Holocaust](./Night%20City%20Holocaust.md) caused the skies over [Night City](./Night%20City.md) to be tinted red. Supplies were scarce and many parts of [Night City](./Night%20City.md) fell into violence in this time.
