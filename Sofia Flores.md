Sofia Flores, more widely known by her handle H3XX, is a flashy [[Netrunner]] out to prove herself. She signs all her hacks with bright flashy malware, much to the chagrin of her employers who often want her to keep a low profile. Nobody can argue that she doesn't get results, though: H3XX is a talented runner who wants everyone across the Net to know her name.
