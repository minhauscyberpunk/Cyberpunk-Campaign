Damage dealt using this ammunition cannot cause a Critical Injury. Additionally, attacks made with this ammunition cannot ablate armor. If damage dealt by this ammunition would reduce a target with more than 1 HP to less than 0 HP, they are instead left at 1 HP.

Arrows, Bullets, and Slugs. Note: Shotguns have been moved to their own types for slugs vs shells.

Cost is per 10 Arrows/Bullets.
