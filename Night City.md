# Description
Night City is a Free City in [[North America]]. Since the [[Reunification War]], it has been surrounded on all sides by [[NUSA|the NUSA]].

Night City is a vibrant and violent city, with districts living in poverty alongside districts of bustling markets and cultural hubs alongside high rise apartments and mansions of the rich and famous.

Night City is the setting of [[Our Story]].
