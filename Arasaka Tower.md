# Description
Araska Tower is a tower in [[Corporate Plaza]] in the [[City Center]] of [[Night City]] and the regional headquarters of [[Arasaka]]. In 2023, [[Night City Holocaust|a nuclear bomb was detonated in Arasaka Tower, (temporarily) destroying it]]. It was rebuilt after the 2050s.
