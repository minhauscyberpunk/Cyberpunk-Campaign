In [[2072]], (former) corpo-kid runaway [[Maple Altaniin Corsair|Crash]] founded a new band in [[Little China]], partly as an outlet to vent her anger at corpos like [[Cedar Corsair|her mother]] and to mourn the loss of [[Altan Khulan|her father]]. [[8.20.2023]] (fans refer to the band as "August 20" or just "August" for short) achieved mild local success before falling apart due to a rift between Crash and [[Crash's Bandmate|a bandmate]]. In its short lifespan, August 20 found a fan in the would-be [[Fixer|fixer]] [[August]], then something of an unknown in [[Night City]], probably in no small part due to his name.
