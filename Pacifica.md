# Description
Pacifica is a district in the south of [[Night City]]. It is home to the sub-districts of [[Coastview]] and [[West Wind Estate]], as well as the active [[Combat Zone]], [[Dogtown]].

Pacifica was once intended to be a seaside jewel of [[Night City]] and a playground of the city's elite: however, plans fell through and the district fell into disrepair and poverty.
