When using this ammunition, whenever you cause the Foreign Object Critical Injury, the victim rolls again on the Critical Injury table (see pg. 187 of Cyberpunk Red Rulebook) until they roll a Critical Injury that isn't Foreign Object. The victim then suffers that Critical Injury as well. This second injury deals no Bonus Damage.

Shotguns only.

Cost is per 10 Slugs.
