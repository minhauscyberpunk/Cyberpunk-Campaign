# Description
The City Center is a district in the middle of [[Night City]]. It contains the subdistricts of [[Corporate Plaza]] and [[Downtown]].

City Center is the business heart of [[Night City]], and some of the world's most powerful corporations have regional headquarters in [[Corporate Plaza]].
