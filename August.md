August is a [Fixer](fixer.md). Operates in [Watson](watson.md)/[Little China](little-china.md). Operates out of [The Colosseum](the-colosseum.md), a warehouse fight club by day and music venue by night, located on the boundary of [Little China](little-china.md) and [Northside Industrial District](northside-industrial-district.md).

August knows [Character Sheet - Crash](character-sheet-crash.md) because she has played at [The Colosseum](the-colosseum.md).
