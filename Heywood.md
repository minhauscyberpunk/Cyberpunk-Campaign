# Description
Heywood is a district in the middle of [[Night City]], just south of [[City Center|the City Center]]. It contains the sub-districts of [[Wellsprings]], [[The Glen]], and [[Vista Del Rey]].

Heywood occupies the area of the city that was Old [[Little China]] before the [[Time of the Red]], during which the area was a [[Combat Zone]]. Since the redevelopments of the 2050s, Heywood was moved from across the river where [[Arroyo]] now stands.
