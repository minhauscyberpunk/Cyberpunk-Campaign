# Description
Nepal is a country in [[South Asia]]. It is a mountainous country, high in the [[Himalayas]], wedged between the world's two most populated countries, [[China]] and [[India]].

The language spoken in Nepal is primarily [[Nepali]].

Nepal borders [[India]] to the south, and [[China]] to the north.
