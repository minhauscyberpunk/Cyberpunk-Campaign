August is a [Fixer](../../../4%20Rules%20and%20Mechanics/Roles/Fixer.md). Operates in [Watson](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson.md)/[Little China](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson/Little%20China.md). Operates out of [The Colosseum](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson/Northside%20Industrial%20District/The%20Colosseum.md), a warehouse fight club by day and music venue by night, located on the boundary of [Little China](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson/Little%20China.md) and [Northside Industrial District](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson/Northside%20Industrial%20District.md).

August knows [Character Sheet - Crash](../../../2%20Crew%20Character%20Sheets/Character%20Sheet%20-%20Crash.md) because she has played at [The Colosseum](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Watson/Northside%20Industrial%20District/The%20Colosseum.md).

August is a fan of [8.20.2023](../../Factions/Bands/8.20.2023.md).
