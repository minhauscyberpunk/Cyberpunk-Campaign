August is a [Fixer](4-rules-and-mechanicsrolesfixer.md). Operates in [Watson](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatson.md)/[Little China](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatsonlittle-china.md). Operates out of [The Colosseum](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatsonnorthside-industrial-districtthe-colosseum.md), a warehouse fight club by day and music venue by night, located on the boundary of [Little China](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatsonlittle-china.md) and [Northside Industrial District](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatsonnorthside-industrial-district.md).

August knows [Character Sheet - Crash](2-crew-character-sheetscharacter-sheet-crash.md) because she has played at [The Colosseum](locationsnorth-americanight-citywatsonnorthside-industrial-districtthe-colosseum.md).
