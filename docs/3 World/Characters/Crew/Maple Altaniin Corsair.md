Maple Altaniin Corsair, better known by her handle, Crash, is the daughter of [Cedar Corsair](../NPCs/Cedar%20Corsair.md), Intelligence Director at [Raven Microcybernetics](Raven%20Microcybernetics.md). Her father, deceased, was [Altan Khulan](../NPCs/Altan%20Khulan.md), making Crash a second-generation [Mongolian](../../Locations/East%20Asia/Mongolia.md)-[American](../../Locations/North%20America.md). She has her mother’s name due a decision made by her mother early in her childhood, that the name “Corsair” would carry more recognition and influence in [Night City](../../Locations/North%20America/Night%20City.md) than “Khulan”.

After the [murder of her father](../../Events/Characters/Murder%20of%20Altan%20Khulan.md), Crash ran away from home, taking [her father's revolver](../../Items/Equipment/Weapons/Ranged%20Weapons/Malorian%20Overture/Big%20Iron.md) and [guitar pick](Crash's%20Guitar%20Pick.md) with her. At age 17 she was a founding member, singer, and guitarist of the band [8.20.2023](../../Factions/Bands/8.20.2023.md) (named after the date of the [Night City Holocaust](../../Events/History/Night%20City%20Holocaust.md)), but found greater local success as a solo artist under the name CrashOut. In the years since she took to the [Rockergirl](../../../4%20Rules%20and%20Mechanics/Roles/Rocker.md) life, she has had a cyberware guitar installed in her arm, which she can play by flexing her tendons. The output jack is in her elbow.

Those more caught up in Corpo circles may know that Maple Corsair is a runaway with a well known last name: few know that she is Crash.

Crash is a crusader when it comes to burning corpo shit; she may go too far in the name of having a bit *too* much fun tearing things down, and may lead others into danger to do so. She wants to open their eyes, so to speak (although anyone looking close enough will see that she probably is just in it for revenge more than ideological consistency).

Aside: Crash may be tamed a little by getting close to someone. Fundamentally her wild side is rooted in a distrust of the people and systems who hurt her: building that back will go a long way to settling her down a little and having her take life more seriously than “Yeah! Let’s break some rules!”
