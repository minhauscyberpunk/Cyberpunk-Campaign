# Description
North Oaks is a sub-district on the north side of [[Westbrook]], in the east of [[Night City]]. North Oaks consist of rolling hills overlooking the city, lined with mansions of the city's most rich and famous.

North Oak is also the location of the [[North Oak Columbarium]].
