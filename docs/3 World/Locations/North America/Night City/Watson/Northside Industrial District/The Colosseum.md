# Description
The Colosseum is a fighting club on the edge of [[Little China]] owned by [[August]]. It is in a warehouse: the lower floor is a series of fight pits, open locker/changing areas, and an improvised bar. Images of gladiators are spray painted on the walls, and a trophy wall/hall of fame sits beside the bar with fake laurel wreaths hanging on nails bearing tags naming victorious fighters. The back half of the warehouse has an upper floor: the stairs run up the side of the warehouse and come to a landing against the back wall, with a door opening to a lounge and office that [[August]] operates from. He can see from his office down to the entire front half of the warehouse.
