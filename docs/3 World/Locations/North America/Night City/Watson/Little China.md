# Description
Little China is a sub-district on the south side of [[Watson]], in [[Night City|Night City's]] inner north. It was an area that underwent massive development in the 2050s, and was the site of [[Night City|Night City's]] first megabuilding, [[Megabuilding H10]]. After this redevelopment, Little China saw growth from the residents of Old Little China, in the area that is now [[Heywood]].

Little China is a bustling but not very well-off sub-district of [[Watson]].
