Asher's Jacket is something of an heirloom passed down to [Character Sheet - Asher](2-crew-character-sheetscharacter-sheet-asher.md) by her parents. It is a [Nepalese](locationssouth-asianepal.md) style jacket that has been modified to suit the [Night City](3-worldlocationsnorth-americanight-citynight-city.md) climate. it has [Kevlar](4-rules-and-mechanicsitemsequipmentarmourkevlar.md) woven inside it, giving it SP7 Armour Rating.

[Character Sheet - Asher](2-crew-character-sheetscharacter-sheet-asher.md) has dyed her jacket pink, and it has patches and details in mountain motifs.
