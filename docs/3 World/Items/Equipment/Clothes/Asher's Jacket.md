Asher's Jacket is something of an heirloom passed down to [Character Sheet - Asher](../../../../2%20Crew%20Character%20Sheets/Character%20Sheet%20-%20Asher.md) by her parents. It is a [Nepalese](../../../Locations/South%20Asia/Nepal.md) style jacket that has been modified to suit the [Night City](3%20World/Locations/North%20America/Night%20City/Night%20City.md) climate. it has [Kevlar](../../../../4%20Rules%20and%20Mechanics/Items/Equipment/Armour/Kevlar.md) woven inside it, giving it SP7 Armour Rating.

[Character Sheet - Asher](../../../../2%20Crew%20Character%20Sheets/Character%20Sheet%20-%20Asher.md) has dyed her jacket pink, and it has patches and details in mountain motifs.
