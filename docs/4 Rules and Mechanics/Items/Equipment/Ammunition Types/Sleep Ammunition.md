When using this ammunition, you deal no damage with your attack. Anyone meat hit by your attack must instead attempt to beat a DV13 Resist Torture/Drugs Check. Anyone who fails is now Prone (see pg. 169 of the Cyberpunk Red Rulebook) and Unconscious for 1 minute or until they are woken by taking damage, or by someone else using an Action that touches them.

Arrows and Grenades.

Cost is per 1 Grenade or per 10 Arrows.
