When using this ammunition, you deal no damage with your attack. Anyone hit by your attack must instead attempt to beat a DV15 Resist Torture/Drugs Check. Anyone who fails suffers the Damaged Eye and Damaged Ear Critical Injuries (see pg. 188) for the next minute. You do not take the Bonus Damage from the Critical Injuries.

Grenades only.

Cost is per 1 Grenade.
