When using this ammunition, whenever you deal damage to a target through their armor, you ignite the target. Until your target spends an Action to put themselves out, they take 2 damage directly to their HP whenever they end their Turn. Multiple instances of this effect cannot stack.

Arrows, Bullets, Grenades, and Shotgun Shells. Note: Shotguns have been moved to their own types for slugs vs shells.

Cost is per 1 Grenade, or per 10 Arrows/Bullets.
