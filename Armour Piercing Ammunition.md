When using this ammunition, you ablate armor by 2 instead of 1 whenever you would ablate armor.

All except Shotgun Shells. Note: Shotguns have been moved to their own types for slugs vs shells.

Cost is per 1 Grenade, per 1 Rocket, or per 10 Arrows/Bullets.
