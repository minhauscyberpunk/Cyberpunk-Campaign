On August 20th 2023, a [Militech](militech.md) incursion team led by [[Morgan Blackhand]] and [[Johnny Silverhand]] infiltrated [Arasaka Tower](arasaka-tower.md) and detonated a tactical nuclear device. The resulting blast killed approximately 4000 people, ended the [[Fourth Corporate War]], and started the [Time of the Red](time-of-the-red.md).
